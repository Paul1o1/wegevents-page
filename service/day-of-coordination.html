Day-of Coordination

You've planned your perfect wedding now let me make sure it happens exactly as you imagined. I take the reins from the morning of, so you can be fully present for every moment.

What day-of coordination includes

Day-of coordination is designed for couples who have planned everything themselves but want a professional to manage the final details and oversee the day. It begins in the weeks leading up to your wedding, when all plans are reviewed, timelines are refined, and every supplier is aligned.

You will hand over the logistics so you can be fully present and enjoy the experience. I step in to coordinate arrivals, manage the schedule, handle any last-minute issues, and ensure everything runs exactly as planned from start to finish.

The result is a wedding day that feels smooth, seamless, and beautifully managed—allowing you, your family, and your guests to relax and enjoy every moment.
