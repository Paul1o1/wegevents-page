The Origin

Born from a Love of Celebration

WEG was born from a deep love of celebration. We carried a lifelong passion for creating an event company and pursued that passion through dedicated learning and rigorous preparation.

Bethel earned her degree in Event Management in Switzerland, and after returning, she shared that refined global expertise with Halaluya and Rakeb as we took the first foundational steps toward building WEG Events.

The name WEG came from our desire to give it an authentic Amharic name that reflects our culture. In Amharic, “Weg” signifies a cherished traditional custom — and one of our richest traditions is coming together in unity to celebrate. Today, we continue building WEG Events into a premier company committed to bringing people together with genuine hospitality.
